# D-0002 — Sub-block light samples follow rendered motion

The approved night-driving work replaces D-0001's whole-block settling only; its vanilla
light lookup integration and immutable published field remain. Sources settle at 1/16
block, with conservative bounds for rounded positive light. Providers may receive the
render partial tick through an additive API. Sampling is capped at 30 Hz before frames;
section requests are deduplicated and height-bounded. The lookup allocates nothing.

Rusty's subsequent correction makes the original stationary light the reference. Preserve
point/line brightness, falloff and the existing vehicle lamp geometry. The experimental
cone and body-pose changes were rejected during this work and are not production changes.

Why: whole-block endpoints make a moving lamp jump even when its vehicle interpolates
smoothly. Sub-block positions and the frame's interpolated position address that cause
without changing the useful broad light pool. The cap limits terrain rebuild pressure.

Validation: pure falloff/settling/bounds tests, the real-client torch/off/dropped/fire
booth, and moving night driving under Sodium, Iris and Complementary. The mod is
client-only, so a dedicated server cannot validate its rendering. Measure frame time
separately from opt-in rebuild-scheduling metrics; scheduling time excludes meshing.
New artifacts remain held for release and retain their existing development version.
